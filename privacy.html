Daily Planner: privacy

Daily Planner is a personal app used by one person, its author.

  • No accounts, no tracking, no ads, no analytics. This website only serves the app's files.
  • Your planner data stays on your phone (the browser's local storage). It is never sent to this website or to anyone else.
  • Whoop data (recovery, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, workouts) is read through Whoop's official API by a program on the owner's own laptop, kept on that laptop, and sent only to the owner's phone over the home Wi-Fi. It is never uploaded anywhere else, sold or shared.
  • Whoop access can be removed at any time in the Whoop app or Whoop's developer dashboard; deleting the laptop's sync files removes the downloaded data.
